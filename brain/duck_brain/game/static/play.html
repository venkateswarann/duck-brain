🦆 Liar's Duck

The duck deals and judges. Lie well. Quack loudly.
How to play
On your turn, play 1–4 cards face down and claim they're all the rank the duck asks for. You can lie! Anyone can hit QUACK! to call a bluff. Caught lying? You take the pile. Wrong call? You take it. First to empty their hand wins.
